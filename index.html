Frequently asked questions

Is registration free?

Yes. Registration for the webinar is free.

Is vendor onboarding free?

Founding-phase onboarding is free. Vendors must complete their profiles and may be subject to review before listings are published. Any future paid plan will be explained and require the vendor’s agreement.

Will everyone get to pitch?

No. Pitch requests are reviewed and selected participants will receive instructions. Everyone can still attend.

Will I definitely get a customer?

No. The event offers an opportunity for visibility and introductions, not guaranteed sales.

How will I receive the joining link?

Joining details will be shared through the contact channels provided once the event arrangements are confirmed.
